## CHECKLIST FOR PREPARING YOUR SUBMISSION

When submitting a manuscript to the journal, authors must confirm that it complies with all the requirements listed below. If the submitted work does not comply with these requirements, the editorial board will be forced to return the materials to the authors for revision.

It is important that this manuscript has not been previously published in whole or in part in the language in which it is submitted to the editorial office, or in any other language, and has not been submitted for consideration to the editorial offices of other journals (or the necessary explanations are provided in the comments for the editor below).

- All authors of the manuscript must review its content. No copyrights should be infringed. All authors of the article must meet the criteria for authorship.
- Any conflict of interest must be indicated in the manuscript in the appropriate section.
- Any use of artificial intelligence must be indicated in the manuscript in the appropriate section.
- Artificial intelligence should not be used to write any of the substance of the manuscript. The use of AI for technical tasks (e.g., grammar checking, etc.) is permitted. In this case, the author(s) of the manuscript must additionally check this and indicate it in the appropriate section of the article text.
- The uniqueness of the text (plagiarism check) must be at least **75%**.
- The manuscript must not contain defamatory, derogatory, or illegal statements.

## REQUIREMENTS FOR MANUSCRIPT FORMAT

- The article must be submitted in Ukrainian or **English (only for foreigners)**.
- The document must be submitted in Microsoft Word format.
- The text should be typed in **10-point font with 1 line spacing**; all illustrations, graphs, and tables should be placed directly in the text where they belong (not at the end of the document).
- A single font style must be used throughout the work to denote similar variables in the text and formulas, etc.
- The main text (without abstracts and references to sources of information, without division into columns) must be **at least 8 pages** long.
- The title of the article must strictly correspond to the content of the manuscript and **not contain abbreviations**.
- It is important that the abstract (number of characters — **from 1900 to 2200**) be structured (**Subject... Purpose... Objectives... Methods... Results... Conclusions...**) and not contain abbreviations that are only understandable from the context of the article.
- Keywords (no more than **10 words**) should be separated by a semicolon (`;`) and should not contain abbreviations that are only understandable from the context of the article.
- Formulas and all symbols in the text must be typed in the **MathType formula editor**. It is prohibited to use graphic objects, tables, and the Word formula editor to type formulas. References to formulas in the article should look like this: (1), (2)–(4). Formulas must be numbered. Numbering should be aligned to the right. A formula is part of the text, so it should be followed by a punctuation mark: if a new sentence follows, then a period; if an explanation follows, then a comma.
- Figures should be created in **Visio** and/or submitted in **.jpg, .jpeg, .png format (no less than 300 dpi)**. The inscriptions on the figures should be clear and easy to read, and the lines of the figure should not be blurred. There should be no noise in the figure. Images, photorealistic images, diagrams, and figures generated by artificial intelligence should be marked with the note **"Imagined with AI."**
- The total number of pages containing figures and tables should **not exceed 3**.
- The table header should not contain empty cells. All tables should be placed vertically.
- Only the following quotation marks should be used in the article: **`" "`**.

### References quotas

- Internet links in the text should be accompanied by complete, correct, real URL addresses;
- the list of references should be presented in **Latin (References)** and use **Harvard Style (BSI)**;
- the number of references to sources of information should be at least **15**;
- the number of references to own publications compared to the total number of references in the article (self-citation) should not exceed **30%**;
- the number of references to foreign English-language publications compared to the total number of references in the article should be at least **40%**;
- the number of recent references (**≤ 4 years and including the current year**) compared to the total number (only published) — not less than **30%**;
- the number of references with **DOI** compared to the total number of references in the article (based on the submitted materials) — not less than **90%**;
- the number of references to articles in **Scopus and/or WoS** journals compared to the total number of references in the article (based on the submitted materials) — not less than **60%**.

### Other

- If AI was used to select literature, the author(s) must check the correctness of the references (their authenticity and correct formatting) before submitting the manuscript.
- References to non-peer-reviewed periodicals published online (based on the journal's website) are prohibited.
- In an article devoted to the development of a device, the results of the research must be provided; if a model/method is proposed, then it is necessary to compare it with others or describe the verification of its functioning (the conditions of the experiment must be clearly defined and described in detail, and the values of the studied parameters must be accurately justified in the text).
- Information about the author(s) of the article must be provided at the end of the article in **Ukrainian and English**, including: surname, first name, and patronymic; academic degree, academic title; place of work (study), position; city, country, email; ORCID ID; SCOPUS ID; **contact phone number**.

### Conflict of interest

It is necessary to indicate the absence or presence of a conflict of interest. If there is a conflict of interest, it must be specified. If there is no conflict of interest, the phrase must be indicated:

> *The authors declare that they have no conflicts of interest, including financial, personal, copyright, or any other conflicts that could influence the research or the results published in this article.*

### Funding

The author(s) must indicate the sources of funding. If there is no funding, this must be stated:

> *The study was conducted without financial support.*

### Data availability

You should choose one of the options below and submit it in the text of the manuscript:

- *The manuscript has linked data in a data repository.*
- *The manuscript contains data in the form of electronic supplementary material.*
- *Data will be provided upon reasonable request.*
- *Data cannot be provided for reasons stated in the data availability statement.*
- *The manuscript has no linked data.*

### Use of artificial intelligence tools

You should choose one of the options below and submit it in the text of the manuscript:

1. If AI is not used, the phrase must be specified:

   > *The authors confirm that they did not use artificial intelligence technology to write this work.*

2. If AI is used, it must be declared in accordance with the editorial policy as follows:
   - AI model and number;
   - where exactly it was used (in which section of the article);
   - what exactly was done using AI tools (specific description);
   - how the authors verified the results provided by AI tools;
   - how the result provided by the AI tool influenced the conclusions of the study.

## REQUIREMENTS FOR FORMATTING THE REFERENCE LIST — Harvard Style (BSI)

1. The entry should always begin with the author's surname. If there are several authors, information about all authors should be provided.
2. After the surname, there should be a **comma, a space, and one initial** (for example: `Verner, N.`).
3. Next, **in parentheses**, you need to indicate the **year**, followed by a comma after the closing parenthesis. For example: `(2025),`
4. **Commas should be used** to separate the elements of the entry.
5. **The title of a book or journal should always be italicized.**
6. **Parts of publications, such as book chapters and journal articles, should be enclosed in quotation marks (`" "`).**

> **It is essential to add the DOI code to articles only if it is available.**

### Sample formats

**Book:**

```
Turner, A. (2006), Introduction to Neogeography, O'Reilly Media, London, 56 p.
```

**Periodical article:**

```
Fesenko, V., Fesenko, N. (2025), "Methods of managing traffic distribution in
information and communication networks of critical infrastructure systems",
Innovative Technologies and Scientific Solutions for Industries, No. 2 (2),
pp. 48–55. DOI: https://doi.org/10.30837/2522-9818.2025.2.048
```

**Thesis:**

```
Heloz, V. (2022), "Legal regulation of income payments: dissertation", Kyiv, 215 p.
```

**Electronic resource:**

```
Serdyuk, T. "Self-regulation in Ukraine: advantages and disadvantages in the current
economic conditions", available at: http://economy.kpi.ua/ru/node/343
(last accessed 23.03.2025).
```

> **Please note!** Failure to comply with the above requirements may delay the editorial preparation of the article.
