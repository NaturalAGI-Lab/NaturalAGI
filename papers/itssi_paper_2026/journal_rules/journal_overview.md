## Scope (English summary)

The journal covers two main streams — engineering sciences and economics. Topics include: information technologies and management systems; sustainable management of projects and programmes; artificial intelligence; energy and transport; electronics; nanotechnologies; computer-integrated technologies; robotics; information-measurement systems; biomedical engineering; management in economic-ecological systems; organisation and control of production processes; contemporary technical means, complexes and systems; enterprise economics; circular economy; project management; mathematical modelling of economic processes; human-resource management; marketing; and adjacent areas. The journal accepts only previously-unpublished original work that has not been submitted elsewhere and which has principled scientific, theoretical and practical significance.

## What the journal does NOT publish

- Patents, copyright certificates, inventions and other IP objects per se
- Idea proposals or short notes
- Popular-science articles
